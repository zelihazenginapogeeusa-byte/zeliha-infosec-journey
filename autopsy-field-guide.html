Autopsy Field Guide

Digital forensics on the disk itself — what's left sitting still on a host after the network traffic is gone and the process list has moved on. Organized by what each ingest module or view surfaces, why it's worth checking, what a suspicious value looks like, and exactly how to pull it up.
