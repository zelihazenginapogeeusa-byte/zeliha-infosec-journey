# 1. What Is a SOC Home Lab?

## What Is a SOC?

A SOC (Security Operations Center) is the team/function that monitors an organization for cyber threats around the clock, detecting, investigating, and responding to them.

## What Does a SOC Analyst L1 Do?

- Monitors alerts from security tooling
- Investigates suspicious activity
- Validates and prioritizes incidents (triage)
- Documents findings
- Escalates critical incidents to L2
- Closes false positives

## Why Do Companies Use a SIEM?

A SIEM (Security Information and Event Management) collects logs from every system, analyzes them, and raises alerts for suspicious activity. Without centralized visibility, spotting attack traces scattered across disparate logs is nearly impossible.

## Why Do We Need a Home Lab?

A home lab lets you experience real SOC operations, tooling, and attack simulations in a safe, isolated environment — without risking production systems or real corporate infrastructure. You'll:

- See exactly how log flow works
- Understand how a SIEM actually generates alerts
- Experience real attack techniques (in a controlled way)
- Build your investigation and reporting skills

## What Will You Learn by the End of This Lab?

- How real attackers actually target systems
- How logs are generated
- How a SIEM detects suspicious activity
- How to investigate alerts step by step
- How to decide: close or escalate to L2
- How to write a professional incident report

## Difficulty Level

Beginner — no prior SOC/SIEM experience required, though basic networking and Windows/Linux knowledge helps.
